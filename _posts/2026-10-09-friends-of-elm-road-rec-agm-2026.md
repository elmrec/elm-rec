Everyone is welcome to join the Friends of Elm Road Recreation Ground AGM on Wednesday 21 October 2026. It is a chance to hear what the group has been working on, help elect the committee, and talk about plans for the year ahead.

![Friends of Elm Road Rec AGM 2026 poster](/assets/images/events/agm-2026/agm-2026-poster-portrait.png)

## Event Details

### When & Where
- Date: Wednesday 21 October 2026
- Time: 7:30pm
- Location: Canbury Pavilion, Canbury Gardens, KT2 5AJ

## Purpose of the AGM

At the AGM we will:

- Re-elect or elect committee members
- Report on activities from the last year
- Communicate plans for next year

## Get Involved

Friends of Elm Road Recreation Ground is a community-led organisation dedicated to maintaining and improving Elm Road Recreation Ground. Through volunteer events, conservation efforts, and community activities, we work together to keep our park beautiful and welcoming for all.

If you would like to get involved, you do not need to attend the meeting. Please use our [contact page](/contact/) to get in touch, or join our [WhatsApp group](https://chat.whatsapp.com/FZ9KMjIYmGNIgRolTgUVxb) for updates about activities and events.
